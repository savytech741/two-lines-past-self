Two lines to a past self: line one warns against deleting a voicemail a father left at 11:47 p.m. on a birthday — laughing, forgetting what he called about, promising to ring again tomorrow — and line two traces why that one ordinary message mattered, because he never rang again and it was the last time his voice was ever heard, which is why every ordinary message gets saved now.
